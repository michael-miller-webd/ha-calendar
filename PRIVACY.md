# Privacy Policy

This OAuth app is used only by its owner to connect their own Google Calendar to their self-hosted Home Assistant instance.

- Calendar data is read by Home Assistant running on the owner's private home network and is not sent to any third party.
- No data is collected, sold, or shared.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
